1． B
【解析】this time tomorrow 表示将来某时间点，用将来进行时 will be doing。
2． B
【解析】be good at 意为「擅长」，固定搭配。
3． A
【解析】回答是时间段「About twenty minutes」，故用 How long 提问。
4． B
【解析】if 引导条件状语从句，主将从现，从句用一般现在时。
5． B
【解析】so + adj. + that 引导结果状语从句。such 后接名词短语。
6． B
【解析】「某地有某物」用 there be 句型，复数用 are there。
7． B
【解析】tell sb. not to do sth. 告诉某人不要做某事。
8． B
【解析】would like to do sth. 想要做某事。
9． C
【解析】when I got home 表示过去某时间点，用过去进行时。
10． B
【解析】The population of ... 作主语时谓语用单数 is。
11． B
【解析】「我也没去过」用 Neither have I，表示否定呼应。
12． A
【解析】ask sb. to do sth. 要求某人做某事。
13． B
【解析】How long 提问时，短暂性动词 bought 需改为延续性动词 had。
14． B
【解析】It's + adj. + for sb. + to do sth. 是固定句型，描述事情的性质用 for。
15． C
【解析】回应感谢用 You're welcome（不用谢）。
16． A
【解析】Last summer 表示过去，叙述用一般过去时 walked。
17． B
【解析】在山上能看到许多鸟和其他「动物」，且指代前面 birds 的同类，用 animals。
18． C
【解析】see sb. doing sth. 看见某人正在做某事。
19． B
【解析】for + 时间段，表示持续四十多年。
20． A
【解析】系动词 was + 形容词 moved（感动的），构成被动意义的形容词。
21． C
【解析】small 与 clean 之间是转折关系，用 but。
22． A
【解析】help sb. (to) do sth. 帮助某人做某事。
23． B
【解析】宾语从句表示「是否」，用 if。
24． A
【解析】will + 动词原形，用 know。
25． B
【解析】主语 It 为第三人称单数，一般现在时用 reminds。

32． 范文： My Best Friend I have a good friend. His name is Li Ming. He is 14 years old. Li Ming is tall and strong. He has short black hair and big eyes. He is friendly and helpful. He often helps others. Everyone likes him. He likes sports very much. He is good at basketball and he plays it every weekend. He also likes reading books in his free time. We have been friends for many years. We study together and play together. I think he is really a good friend. I hope our friendship will last forever.
【解析】评分标准：内容完整4分，语言准确4分，书写规范2分。词数明显不足或过多酌减。
33． 范文： How to Keep Healthy Health is very important to us. How can we keep healthy? Here are some suggestions. First, we should eat healthy food. We'd better eat more fruit and vegetables, and eat less junk food. Junk food is bad for our health. Second, we should do exercise every day. We can run, swim or play ball games. Exercise makes us strong and happy. Third, we need to get enough sleep. We should sleep for at least eight hours every night. Finally, we should keep a good mood. Smiling is the best medicine. When we feel stressed, we should learn to relax. If we follow these suggestions, I believe we will be healthier and happier.
【解析】评分标准： 一类（17—20分）：要点齐全，语言流畅，语法正确，衔接自然。 二类（13—16分）：要点较全，语言基本通顺，有少量语法错误。 三类（9—12分）：要点基本齐全，语言有较多错误，但能达意。 四类（8分以下）：要点不全，语言错误多，影响理解。
