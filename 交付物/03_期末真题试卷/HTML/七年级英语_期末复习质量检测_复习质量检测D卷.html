1． 【A篇参考】 Sam is a middle school student. He is from Australia. Now he lives in Hubei with his parents. He likes Chinese food. He often has rice and vegetables for lunch. He can speak a little Chinese. He goes to school by bike. He has many Chinese friends. He often plays basketball with them after school. He likes China very much. 判断： 1. Sam is from Australia. (T) 2. Sam lives in Hubei now. (T) 3. Sam doesn't like Chinese food. (F) 4. Sam goes to school on foot. (F) 5. Sam has many Chinese friends. (T)
【解析】判断题要在原文中找到对应依据。
2． 【B篇参考】 There is a big shop near my home. It's called Sunshine Shop. It opens at eight in the morning and closes at nine in the evening. You can buy many things there, such as food, drinks, school things and clothes. The things in the shop are good and cheap. The shopkeepers are very friendly. Many people like shopping there. I often buy some books and pens there. 选择： 1. What's the name of the shop? (A. Sunshine Shop. B. Sun Shop.) → A 2. When does the shop open? (A. At 8 a.m. B. At 9 a.m.) → A 3. What can you buy there? (A. Food and clothes. B. Only books.) → A 4. What are the things in the shop like? (A. Good and cheap. B. Bad and expensive.) → A 5. How are the shopkeepers? (A. Friendly. B. Strict.) → A
【解析】抓住商店的基本信息（名称、时间、商品、服务）。
