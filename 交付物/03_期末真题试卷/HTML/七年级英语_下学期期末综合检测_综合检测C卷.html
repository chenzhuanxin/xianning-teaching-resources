一、单项选择（每题1分，共15分）
1．[天气] — What's the weather like today?　— ________ （1分）
A. It's sunny.　B. It's Monday.　C. It's June 1st.　D. It's ten.
2．[疑问词] — ________ is it from here to the bank?　— About five minutes' walk. （1分）
A. How long　B. How far　C. How often　D. How many
3．[将来时] There ________ a basketball match next week. （1分）
A. is going to have　B. is going to be　C. are going to be　D. will have
4．[一般过去时] — Did you go to the zoo yesterday?　— No, I ________. （1分）
A. don't　B. didn't　C. wasn't　D. haven't
5．[非谓语] The teacher told us ________ hard. （1分）
A. study　B. to study　C. studying　D. studied
6．[交际] — Would you like to go swimming with me?　— ________ （1分）
A. Yes, I'd love to.　B. Yes, I do.　C. No, I can't.　D. You're welcome.
7．[介词] My hometown is ________ the south of China. （1分）
A. on　B. in　C. at　D. to
8．[疑问词] — How ________ is the book?　— It's twenty yuan. （1分）
A. much　B. many　C. long　D. far
9．[非谓语] It's important ________ English well. （1分）
A. learn　B. to learn　C. learning　D. learns
10．[介词] The boy ________ short hair is my cousin. （1分）
A. in　B. with　C. of　D. on
11．[一般过去时] — What happened to you?　— I ________ my leg. （1分）
A. hurt　B. hurts　C. hurting　D. to hurt
12．[介词短语] She is good ________ singing. （1分）
A. at　B. in　C. for　D. with

A. a few　B. a little　C. many　D. few
15．[交际] — Thank you for helping me.　— ________ （1分）
A. Not at all.　B. Don't say so.　C. That's right.　D. Yes, please.

1． 【参考短文】 It was Sunday yesterday. The weather was fine. Li Ming and his friends went to the park. They played games and had a picnic there. Li Ming took many photos. They saw some birds singing in the trees. Everyone had a good time. In the afternoon, they went home at five. Li Ming was tired but very happy.
【解析】完形填空注意前后文逻辑与动词时态。
1． 【A篇参考】 Beijing is the capital of China. It is a very old city. There are many places of interest in Beijing, such as the Great Wall, the Palace Museum and Tian'anmen Square. Every year, millions of people come to visit Beijing. The weather in Beijing is different in four seasons. It is hot in summer and cold in winter. Autumn is the best season to visit Beijing. 判断： 1. Beijing is the capital of China. (T) 2. There are few places of interest in Beijing. (F) 3. The weather in Beijing is hot in winter. (F) 4. Autumn is the best season to visit Beijing. (T) 5. Millions of people come to Beijing every year. (T)
【解析】判断题要核对原文的肯定与否定表述。
2． 【B篇参考】 Hello! I'm Wang Lin. I live in a small village in the countryside. There is a river in front of my house. The water is clean and there are many fish in it. Behind my house, there is a big mountain. We can see many trees and flowers on the mountain. In spring, the mountain is green and beautiful. I like my village very much. It is quieter and more beautiful than the city. 选择： 1. Where does Wang Lin live? (A. In a city. B. In a village.) → B 2. What's in front of his house? (A. A river. B. A mountain.) → A 3. What's behind his house? (A. A river. B. A mountain.) → B 4. What's the village like in spring? (A. Green and beautiful. B. White and cold.) → A 5. Which does Wang Lin like better? (A. The city. B. His village.) → B
【解析】抓住方位词与比较关系。
3． 【C篇参考】 Dear Li Hua, How are you? I'm writing to tell you about my summer holiday plan. I'm going to visit Beijing with my parents. We will go there by train. We are going to visit the Great Wall and the Palace Museum. I'm also going to try some Chinese food. I hope to see you in Beijing. Please write back soon. Yours, Tom 回答： 1. Who is Tom going to Beijing with? → His parents. 2. How will they go there? → By train. 3. What places are they going to visit? → The Great Wall and the Palace Museum. 4. What is Tom also going to try? → Some Chinese food. 5. Who is Tom writing to? → Li Hua.
【解析】书信类阅读要注意写信人与收信人。
1． 1. Great　2. dangerous　3. work　4. swimming　5. drawing
【解析】根据首字母与语境判断。
2． 1. visited　2. are swimming　3. playing　4. was　5. better
【解析】注意一般过去时、现在进行时、固定搭配与比较级。
3． 1. Did; go　2. What; doing　3. will not / won't　4. What an　5. not short
【解析】句型转换注意时态与固定句式。
4． 1. went　2. am going to　3. was; like　4. didn't do　5. with me
【解析】翻译注意时态与固定搭配。
1． 【范文】 My Favourite Season My favourite season is spring. Spring comes in March, April and May. In spring, the weather is warm and the days are longer. The trees turn green and the flowers come out. Everything comes back to life. I like flying kites with my friends in the park. I can also go hiking and enjoy the beautiful scenery. Spring is full of hope. I love spring best because it makes me happy and energetic.
【解析】突出季节特点与个人感受，语言丰富。
