一、单项选择（每题1分，共15分）
1．[交际] — Nice to meet you.　— ________ （1分）
A. Nice to meet you, too.　B. Good morning.　C. Thank you.　D. Fine, thanks.
2．[物主代词] — What's ________ name?　— My name is Kangkang. （1分）
A. you　B. your　C. yours　D. yourself
3．[冠词] This is ________ apple. It's very sweet. （1分）
A. a　B. an　C. the　D. /
4．[be动词] — Where ________ you from?　— I'm from China. （1分）
A. is　B. am　C. are　D. be
5．[主谓一致] Jane ________ long hair and big eyes. （1分）
A. have　B. has　C. is　D. are
6．[疑问词] — ________ is your schoolbag?　— It's red. （1分）
A. What colour　B. How many　C. How old　D. What time
7．[There be] There ________ a pen and two books on the desk. （1分）
A. is　B. are　C. have　D. has
8．[一般现在时] — What ________ your father do?　— He is a doctor. （1分）
A. do　B. does　C. is　D. are
9．[现在进行时] Look! The children ________ football on the playground. （1分）
A. play　B. plays　C. are playing　D. played
10．[疑问词] — ________ do you go to school?　— By bike. （1分）
A. What　B. How　C. Where　D. When
11．[主谓一致] My mother often ________ TV in the evening. （1分）
A. watch　B. watches　C. watching　D. is watching
12．[some/any] — Would you like ________ tea?　— Yes, please. （1分）
A. some　B. any　C. many　D. a
13．[介词短语] The library is ________ the classroom building. （1分）
A. next　B. next to　C. next of　D. near to
14．[名词性物主代词] — Whose book is this?　— It's ________. （1分）
A. she　B. her　C. hers　D. she's
15．[交际] — Thank you very much.　— ________ （1分）
A. You're welcome.　B. Thank you.　C. That's right.　D. All right.

1．[阅读理解] （A）阅读短文，判断正（T）误（F）。（每小题2分，共10分） （10分）
2．[阅读理解] （B）阅读短文，选择最佳答案。（每小题2分，共10分） （10分）
3．[任务型阅读] （C）阅读短文，回答问题。（每小题2分，共10分） （10分）
四、词汇与句型（共30分）
1．[词汇] （一）根据句意及首字母提示，补全单词。（每题1分，共5分） 1. My family name is Li. I'm a Chinese s________. 2. — What's the w________ like today? — It's sunny. 3. My brother likes l________ to music. 4. There are twelve m________ in a year. 5. She goes to school on f________. （5分）
2．[词形变化] （二）用所给词的适当形式填空。（每题1分，共5分） 1. My sister ________ (have) a nice bag. 2. Look! They ________ (play) games. 3. There are five ________ (tomato) on the table. 4. This is ________ (I) new pen. 5. He often ________ (go) to school by bus. （5分）
3．[句型转换] （三）按要求完成句子。（每题2分，共10分） 1. My name is Jane. (对划线部分提问) ________ ________ your name? 2. He is a teacher. (改为一般疑问句) ________ he a teacher? 3. This is my book. (改为复数句) ________ are my ________. 4. I like English. (改为否定句) I ________ ________ English. 5. She goes to school at seven. (对划线部分提问) ________ ________ she go to school? （10分）
4．[翻译] （四）根据汉语意思完成句子。（每题2分，共10分） 1. 你的电话号码是多少？ What's your ________ ________? 2. 这是我的父母。 ________ ________ my parents. 3. 他每天早上六点起床。 He ________ ________ at six every morning. 4. 我们班有四十五名学生。 There are ________ ________ students in our class. 5. 你最喜欢的颜色是什么？ What's your ________ ________? （10分）

【解析】完形填空需根据上下文语境选择，注意时态、词性与搭配。
1． 【A篇参考】 Mike is a middle school student in Beijing. He is thirteen years old. He gets up at six thirty every morning. He has breakfast at seven and then goes to school. He has four classes in the morning and two in the afternoon. His favourite subject is math. After school, he likes playing football with his friends. He usually does his homework at seven in the evening. He goes to bed at nine thirty. 判断：1. Mike is thirteen years old. (T) 2. Mike gets up at seven. (F) 3. Mike has six classes a day. (T) 4. Mike's favourite subject is English. (F) 5. Mike goes to bed at nine thirty. (T)
【解析】判断题要回到原文核对细节。
2． 【B篇参考】 Tom has a happy family. His father is a doctor. He works in a big hospital. His mother is a teacher. She teaches English in a middle school. Tom has a sister. Her name is Mary. She is a student. They live in Shanghai. On Sundays, they often go to the park. They like flying kites there. Tom's favourite sport is swimming. Mary likes dancing. 选择： 1. What's Tom's father? (A. A doctor. B. A teacher. C. A driver.) → A 2. Where do they live? (A. Beijing. B. Shanghai. C. Nanjing.) → B 3. What's Tom's favourite sport? (A. Football. B. Swimming. C. Basketball.) → B 4. What does Mary like? (A. Dancing. B. Singing. C. Drawing.) → A 5. When do they often go to the park? (A. On Mondays. B. On Saturdays. C. On Sundays.) → C
【解析】选择题要抓住关键词定位原文。
