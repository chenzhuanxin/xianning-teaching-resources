1． 【A篇参考】 Mary is an American girl. She is in Beijing now. She studies in a middle school. She has many friends in China. She likes Chinese food very much. She often has noodles and dumplings. Her favourite Chinese food is dumplings. She can speak a little Chinese. She wants to learn it well. 判断： 1. Mary is an American girl. (T) 2. Mary is in Shanghai now. (F) 3. Mary likes Chinese food very much. (T) 4. Mary's favourite Chinese food is noodles. (F) 5. Mary can speak Chinese very well. (F)
【解析】判断题回到原文核对细节。
2． 【B篇参考】 It's Sunday today. The weather is fine. Lucy and her family are at the park. Her father is boating on the lake. Her mother is talking with a friend. Her brother is playing football with his friends. Lucy is drawing a picture under a big tree. She is drawing the beautiful lake. They are all having a good time. 选择： 1. What day is it today? (A. Saturday. B. Sunday.) → B 2. Where are Lucy's family? (A. At home. B. At the park.) → B 3. What is Lucy's father doing? (A. Boating. B. Reading.) → A 4. What is Lucy doing? (A. Playing football. B. Drawing.) → B 5. How do they feel? (A. Happy. B. Tired.) → A
【解析】抓住现在进行时的描述，对应人物与动作。
