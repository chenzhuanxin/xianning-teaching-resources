A. do　B. does　C. is　D. are

【解析】对「Would you like...?」的接受回答可以是 Yes, some water, please.
9． C
【解析】have a good time 的进行时：are having a good time。

2． 【B篇参考】 Do you know the Spring Festival? It is the most important festival in China. It usually comes in January or February. Before the festival, people clean their houses and buy new clothes. On the eve of the festival, families get together and have a big dinner. Dumplings are a traditional food. Children can get red packets from their parents. People also set off fireworks. Everyone is very happy. 选择： 1. When does the Spring Festival usually come? (A. In January or February. B. In June.) → A 2. What do people do before the festival? (A. Clean houses and buy new clothes. B. Go to school.) → A 3. What's the traditional food? (A. Dumplings. B. Noodles.) → A 4. What can children get? (A. Red packets. B. New books.) → A 5. How do people feel? (A. Happy. B. Sad.) → A
【解析】抓住节日的时间、习俗与情感。
