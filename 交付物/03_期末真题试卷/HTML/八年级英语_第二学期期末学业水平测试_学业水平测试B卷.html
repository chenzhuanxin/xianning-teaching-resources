It is warm in spring and autumn, hot in summer and not very cold in winter. My hometown is famous for its oranges. Every autumn, many people come here to buy them. 【解析】评分标准：要点完整4分，语言准确4分，书写规范2分。
33． 范文： My Dream Everyone has a dream. My dream is to be a doctor. Why do I want to be a doctor? When I was ten, my grandmother was seriously ill. The doctors saved her life. From then on, I made up my mind to be a doctor. I want to help people who are ill and make them healthy again. To make my dream come true, I will study hard, especially science subjects. I will also read more books about medicine. I believe that if I work hard, my dream will come true one day.
【解析】评分标准： 一类（17—20分）：要点齐全，语言流畅，语法正确，衔接自然。 二类（13—16分）：要点较全，语言基本通顺，有少量语法错误。 三类（9—12分）：要点基本齐全，语言有较多错误，但能达意。 四类（8分以下）：要点不全，语言错误多，影响理解。
