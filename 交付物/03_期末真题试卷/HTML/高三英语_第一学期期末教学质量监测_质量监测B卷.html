1．阅读 1. A　2. B　3. A　4. A
【解析】1. 细节题：原文「Those who volunteered at least twice a month scored higher in communication and problem-solving tests」。 2. 细节题：同上句，频次为「at least twice a month」。 3. 细节题：原文「they also warn that too much volunteer work may take up study time」。 4. 推断题：由「helping others has made me more confident」可推知志愿服务有助增强自信。
2．完形 1. A　2. A　3. A　4. B　5. A　6. A　7. B　8. B　9. A　10. B
【解析】1. easy：她原以为工作容易，与后文形成反差。2. shocked：看到垃圾感到震惊。 3. picking up：捡拾垃圾。4. clean：渔民说从前河水清澈。5. teach：向学生宣传环保。 6. signs：在河边立标牌。7. make a difference：发挥作用、带来改变。8. protected：保护环境。 9. clear：希望河水恢复清澈。10. realise：让更多人认识到保护自然的重要性。
3．语法填空 1. best　2. found　3. to read　4. which　5. more 6. an　7. has grown　8. where　9. opens　10. reading
【解析】1. best：one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词。2. found：When I was in Grade Seven 提示过去时。 3. to read：advise sb. to do sth.。4. which：非限制性定语从句，指代前面整句话。 5. more：than 提示比较级。6. an：half an hour 固定搭配。 7. has grown：since 引导的从句提示现在完成时。8. where：定语从句关系副词，表地点。 9. opens：not only…but also… 连接并列谓语，主语为 Reading，用第三人称单数。 10. reading：spend time doing sth.。

Citizens also play a part: we sort rubbish, plant trees and save electricity. I believe that if everyone does a little, our city will become even greener and more beautiful in the future. Yours, Li Hua （100分）参考范文： My Dream and Hard Work Everyone has a dream, and so do I. My dream is to become a doctor who can help people in need.
