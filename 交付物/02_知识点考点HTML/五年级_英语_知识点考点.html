2025—2026学年五年级英语
1

语音与字母组合

基础必备
音语音规律
  • 字母组合ee/ea：读 /iː/（see, tea, read）。
  • 字母组合oo：读 /uː/（food, moon）或 /ʊ/（book, look）。
  • 字母组合ow/ou：读 /aʊ/（how, house）或 /əʊ/（window, snow）。
  • 字母组合ai/ay：读 /eɪ/（rain, day）。
  • 字母组合igh/ie：读 /aɪ/（high, pie）。
  • 字母组合ar/or/er/ir/ur：/ɑː/（car）、/ɔː/（for）、/ɜː/（her, bird, nurse）。
★
必考：判断单词中划线字母组合的读音是否相同。
2

核心词汇

必背内容
词话题词汇
  • 日常活动：get up, have breakfast, go to school, do homework, watch TV, go to bed, play sports, eat dinner。
  • 周末活动：visit grandparents, go shopping, play the piano, climb mountains, take a dancing class。
  • 方位与地点：in front of, behind, beside, between, near, next to, library, hospital, cinema, bookstore。
  • 月份与节日：January~December；National Day, Children's Day, Spring Festival, Mid-Autumn Day。
  • 季节与天气：spring, summer, autumn, winter；windy, rainy, cloudy, snowy, sunny。
  • 交通方式：by bus, by bike, on foot, by subway, by plane, by ship。
  • 职业：teacher, doctor, nurse, driver, farmer, policeman, cook, singer, writer。
★
必考：看图选词、单词分类、听音选词、单词拼写。词汇量是五年级英语的关键。
3

常用句型与语法

必考题型
句核心句型
  • 询问时间：When do you...? — I ... at ...。
  • 询问位置：Where is...? — It's next to...。
  • 询问喜好：Do you like...? / Why?
  • 询问能做什么：What can you do? — I can...。
  • 询问季节：Which season do you like best? — I like...best.
  • 询问交通方式：How do you go to school? — By bus.
  • 询问职业：What does he/she do? — He/She is a...。
  • 询问在做什么：What is he/she doing? — He/She is ...。
✎
语法要点：一般现在时（第三人称单数动词加s/es）；现在进行时（be+doing）；名词复数；形容词比较级初步；there be 句型（就近原则）。
4

情景交际

语言运用
用功能话题
  • 约定与邀请：Let's... / Would you like to...?
  • 问路与指路：Excuse me. Where is...? / Go straight. Turn left/right.
  • 打电话：Hello, this is... speaking. / Can I speak to...?
  • 购物：Can I help you? / How much...? / I'd like...。
  • 表达感受：It's great! / That's too bad. / Sounds good.
★
必考：补全对话、选择恰当应答、情景匹配。要能根据场合选出得体的表达。
